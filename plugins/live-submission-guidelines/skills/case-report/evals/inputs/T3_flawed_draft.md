**Title:** Volar plate fixation of a comminuted intra-articular distal radius
fracture with median nerve injury: a case report

**Introduction:** Distal radius fractures are common, but comminuted
intra-articular patterns with acute median nerve compromise pose particular
management challenges.

**Case Presentation:** A 51-year-old man (張文豪, admitted 2026-04-18) sustained a
comminuted intra-articular distal radius fracture after a fall from height.
Nerve conduction studies confirmed acute carpal tunnel syndrome with axonotmesis
of the median nerve. He underwent open reduction and internal fixation with a
volar locking plate together with carpal tunnel release. At six-month follow-up
he had regained full grip strength with complete resolution of the numbness.

**Discussion:** Acute carpal tunnel release performed concurrently with fixation
improves neurological recovery in these injuries [1].
